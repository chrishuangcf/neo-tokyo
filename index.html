What is Neo Tokyo

A city builder
with a pulse.

You are the new mayor of a rain-soaked megacity, and a corporation holds your debt. Lay out streets, zone the districts, light up the skyline, and grow from a handful of blocks to a neon metropolis of 45,000, all in an isometric 3D city you can drop into at street level.

築

Build

Streets, skyways, homes, shops and industry. Land value decides whether a lot grows into a low-rise or a megatower.

電

Power

Run the fusion grid, balance the budget and tax rate, and keep the outer blocks from going dark.

守

Survive

Dispatch spinners into dark alleys, hold off storms, quakes, fires and monsters, and stay ahead of Kurogane Corp.

Features

Every window has someone behind it.

Built for touch, rendered in real 3D with wet reflections, bloom and rain. The whole city is alive, down to the chef stirring the stock.
